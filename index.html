How to play

Fill each row, column, and 3 × 3 box with the digits 1–9, without repeats.

  • Make your move

    Select a white cell and type a number. Type another digit to replace an answer. Shaded starting numbers stay locked.

  • Think in pencil

    Turn Notes on to keep possible digits in empty cells. Type a candidate again to remove it; turn Notes off to enter an answer.

  • Spot the matches

    Select an answer or starting number to highlight its matches. Empty cells clear highlights; pencil notes do not count.

  • Room to rethink

    Undo reverses the latest answer or note change; Redo restores it. A new edit clears Redo. Reset asks before clearing your entries, notes, and history.

Use Tab to move between editable cells, and Backspace or Delete to clear a cell.
